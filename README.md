## 🚀 How to Use the Offline Companion

You don't need to install Python, run Node.js, or launch local web servers. 

1. **Open the Dashboard**: Simply open **[`dist/index.html`](dist/index.html)** in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, or mobile/tablet browsers).
2. **Second-Screen Ready**: Keep it open on a second monitor, tablet, or phone alongside your game.
3. **100% Offline & Local**: Works completely without internet. All catches, checklists, and route progress are saved automatically to your browser's local storage.
4. **Backup Anytime**: Export or restore your progress with 1-click JSON backup buttons right in the app header.

## ⚔️ Two Ways to Play: Classic vs. Hard Remix

Whether you prefer the traditional Kanto journey or modern spliced chaos, the walkthrough covers both modes with dedicated route tables, trainer rosters, and boss cards:

| Feature | 🌿 Classic Hard Mode | 🌪️ Hard Remix Mode |

## 🌟 What Makes This Walkthrough Different

### 🛡️ 1. Three Parallel Evolving Starter Tracks
Instead of forcing a single team or showing rigid 6-slot endgame clones from Chapter 1, the guide tracks three parallel playthrough paths:
* **🌱 Team Bulbasaur**: Grass momentum, early sleep powder control, Leech Seed attrition, and bulky defensive cores.
* **🔥 Team Charmander**: High-velocity physical and special wallbreakers, Fighting coverage, and offensive pivots.
* **💧 Team Squirtle**: Bulky balance, Water/Steel/Ground defensive walls, and Regenerator pivots.

> **Organic Team Progression**: Only your starter is a permanent anchor. Every chapter introduces accessible fusions and rotated catches made strictly from Pokémon available on the routes you've visited so far.

### ⚔️ 2. Built-in Gym Duel Limits
Infinite Fusion prompts you to select a matching party size when entering Gym battles. The guide highlights your designated **Top Duelists** (`⭐ GYM DUELIST`) and provides specific matchup tactics:
* **Brock & Misty**: Strict **2 vs 2**
* **Lt. Surge & Erika**: Strict **3 vs 3**
* **Koga, Sabrina & Blaine**: Strict **4 vs 4**
* **Giovanni & Johto Leaders**: Strict **5 vs 5**

### ⚠️ 3. Zero-Missable Alarm System
Pulsing warning cards alert you before you trigger irreversible points of no return:
* **Route 22 Rival Battle**: Must be fought *before* defeating Brock to permanently unlock the **Secret Garden**.
* **Oak's Aide Field Research**: Key milestones for obtaining the **Poké Radar**.
* **Celadon Building Materials**: Must be turned in before the Elite Four before the site permanently converts into the Fighting Arena.
* **Team Rocket Double-Agent Strategy**: Join Team Rocket on Route 24 for the disguise and quests, but report their crimes to Officer Jenny in Cerulean and Celadon for **+10 Karma each**—triggering the police raid and unlocking the **Mythical Diancie** event!

### 🎒 4. All 9 HM Replacement Tools
Never waste a party slot on an HM slave. The guide details the exact locations and requirements for every HM tool:
* **✂️ Shears (Cut)**: Route 2 Before Forest via Diglett's Cave (after obtaining the Boulder Badge).
* **🏮 Lantern (Flash)**: Received at any Hotel after completing 10 Hotel Quests.
* **⛏️ Pickaxe (Rock Smash)**: Vermilion City / Rock Tunnel entrance.
* **🏄 Surfboard (Surf)**: Safari Zone Secret House.
* **✨ Teleport**: Fast-travel field move acts as Fly to any visited Pokémon Center as soon as you earn the Thunder Badge.

### 🧪 5. Built-in Second-Screen Tools
Press hotkeys anytime in the dashboard to open interactive power tools:
* **🧬 Interactive Fusion Lab (<kbd>F</kbd>)**: Calculate base stats, typing, ability inheritance, and legal moves for any spliced Pokémon.
* **🛡️ 18-Type Matchup Matrix (<kbd>M</kbd>)**: Real-time defensive and offensive weakness/resistance calculator for any dual-type combination.
* **📋 Speedrun Cheat Sheet (<kbd>C</kbd>)**: High-level overview of all 25 chapters, level caps, ace Pokémon, duel formats, and key item rewards.
* **👥 My Team Builder (<kbd>T</kbd>)**: Plan, inspect, and verify typing coverage for your active squad.

## 📖 Infinite Fusion Essentials Cheatsheet

Keep these key mechanical differences in mind while playing:

### Evolution Rules
* **Trade Evolutions**: Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler, Haunter, and Phantump evolve naturally at **Level 40 OR using a Linking Cord** from your Bag (not Level 37).
* **Evolution Items**: Metal Coat (Lv. 40), Dragon Scale (Lv. 50), Protector (Lv. 55), and Electirizer/Magmarizer (Lv. 50) evolve naturally by level OR by using the item like an evolution stone from your Bag.
* **No Friendship Grinding**: Happiness evolutions are completely eliminated!
  * **Baby Pokémon** (Pichu, Cleffa, Togepi, etc.): Evolve at **Level 15**.
  * **Buneary**: Evolves at **Level 25**.
  * **Riolu & Munchlax**: Evolve at **Level 30**.
  * **Golbat**: Evolves into Crobat at **Level 40**.
  * **Chansey**: Evolves into Blissey at **Level 42**.

### World Notes
* **S.S. Anne Stays Docked**: Unlike official FireRed, the S.S. Anne **never departs**. You can return anytime for cabin healing, the Krabby legs quest, the waiter quest, the gift Torkoal, and late-game legendaries.
* **Super Splicers**: Unlock at Cerulean Mart ($300 at any Mart). Super Splicers preserve the higher IVs and optimal stats of both parents.

## 🛠️ For Modders & Contributors

The walkthrough content and dashboard are maintained through an automated, test-driven toolchain:

* **Compile Dashboard**: `python scripts/compile_dashboard.py` (bundles sliced chapter JSONs and compresses payload into standalone `dist/index.html`).
* **Run Test Suite**: `python scripts/test_all_chapters.py` (validates level caps, duel sizes, moveset legality, chronological obtainability, and wiki accuracy).
* **Audit Repo Hygiene**: `python scripts/audit_bloat.py` (enforces atomic file budgets and root cleanliness).
* **Content Structure**: Chapter micro-locations live in `chapters/chXX/phases/` and `chapters/chXX/phases_remix/`, keeping individual files modular and human-editable.
